Before you book

Good to know.

What should I do before my first visit?

Come with bare or lightly polished nails if possible — it saves chair time. If you're allergic to any nail products or have had reactions before, mention it when booking so we can patch-test in advance.

How long does a gel extension appointment take?

A full set runs about 90 minutes. Infills are quicker at 60 minutes. We build in a buffer, so we're rarely running behind.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free to reschedule up to 4 hours before your slot. Festive-season and weekend bookings ask for 24 hours' notice since those slots move fast.

Do you accept walk-ins?

We keep a couple of same-day slots open every day, but booking ahead guarantees your preferred technician and time.
