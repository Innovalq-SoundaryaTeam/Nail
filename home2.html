Drop FAQs

Quick answers.

Can I mix finishes across hands?

Yes — plenty of guests do chrome on one hand and cat-eye on the other. Just mention it when booking so we allow extra chair time.

How long does the Diwali Edit run?

Booking is open now through October 20. After that, drop finishes return to standard nail-art pricing on the menu.

Do drop bundles include removal of old gel?

Soak-off of existing gel or extensions is included at no extra charge as part of any full-set booking.
