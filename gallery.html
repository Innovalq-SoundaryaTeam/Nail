How to bring a reference in.

Save it

Screenshot the design here or from Instagram — exact shade matches aren't guaranteed but we'll get close.

Name it at booking

Add the design name to the notes field on the contact page, or mention it when you call.

We quote chair time

Hand-painted art and 3D work take longer — we'll confirm timing and price before you arrive.

Adapt if you like

Want a different base colour or nail shape? Tell your technician — every design here is a starting point.
