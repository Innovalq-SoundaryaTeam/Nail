How it works

Your appointment, step by step.

A two-minute skin and nail check, plus a look at your reference photos or colour ideas.

Shape, cuticle work and buffing — the foundation that makes everything after it last.

Cuticle oil, a hand massage and aftercare notes before you're back out the door.

Good to know

Aftercare & questions.

How do I make my gel manicure last?

Cuticle oil every night, gloves for washing up, and come back for an infill at the 2–3 week mark rather than letting it grow out fully.

Are gel extensions bad for my natural nails?

Not when they're removed properly. We always soak off rather than pry, which is what actually causes damage.

Can I get nail art without a full new set?

Yes — accent nails and add-on art can go over an existing gel manicure, priced per nail from the pricing menu.
